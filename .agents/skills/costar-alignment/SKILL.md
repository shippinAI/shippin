# CO-STAR alignment

Infer context, objective, style, tone, audience and desired response from the
request, prior decisions and relevant project evidence. Label uncertain inferences;
do not treat a framework as proof or force the user to fill out six fields.
Consult available project sources first, then identify decisions whose answers
would materially change the result. For each, explain the practical consequence
in everyday language and offer two or three mutually exclusive options with a
recommended default where reasonable. Permit a free-text answer. Group related
questions and avoid asking about choices the user already made.

Ask early when a decision is genuinely needed, continue independent work while
waiting, and use a stated reasonable assumption for optional questions after a
reasonable opportunity to answer. Do not treat silence as permission for a
dependent action. Save the available request, decisions and relevant answer in the
session record described by `governance/README.md`; record gaps honestly.
